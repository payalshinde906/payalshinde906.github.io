Who I am

I am a Computer Engineering graduate based in Pune, Maharashtra. Over the past six months I worked as a Software Engineering Intern at Preflex Solution Pvt. Ltd., where I contributed to a full-stack credit intelligence platform called CreditDataWatch.

My work there involved backend development with FastAPI and PostgreSQL, building and integrating REST APIs, implementing authentication and role-based access control, and adding application features such as purchase order management and AI-assisted vendor credibility scoring.

I am familiar with Python, JavaScript, React.js, FastAPI, PostgreSQL and Git. I am most interested in backend engineering and in developing business-oriented software applications.
